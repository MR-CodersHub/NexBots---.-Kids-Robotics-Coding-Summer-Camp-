Our Founding Story

Built with Passion in Abuja

CodeCampus International began in 2021 as a weekend robotics club at DBM Plaza in Wuse 2. We observed a glaring gap: while Nigerian kids were avid consumers of smartphones, tablets, and gaming consoles, very few had the chance to understand the logic, code, and hardware sensors driving those technologies.

What began with six children and three Arduino boards has blossomed into Abuja’s premier tech camp, with over 800+ young creators trained across four unforgettable summers.

Our Camp Core Slogan "Build skills. Spark creativity. Shape the future."

Pedagogical Framework

The CodeCampus 4-Stage Methodology

How we guide campers from curious beginners to proud innovators in four progressive phases.

01 Phase 01 • Discovery

Spark & Deconstruct

Deconstructing how everyday gadgets work. Kids take apart safe mechanical toys, trace electrical currents, and analyze how motors turn and sensors read data.

⚙️ Outcome: Systems Curiosity
02 Phase 02 • Construction

Code & Breadboard

Step-by-step pair building. Students write algorithmic scripts in Scratch or Python and wire physical breadboards with guided coach mentorship.

💡 Outcome: Working Prototype
03 Phase 03 • Resilience

Iterate & Debug

Normalizing trial-and-error. When a robot hits a wall, campers analyze telemetry radar data and modify code thresholds until autonomous navigation succeeds.

🔧 Outcome: Growth Mindset
04 Phase 04 • Mastery

Showcase & Pitch

Communication excellence. Campers stand beside their working inventions during Demo Day, explaining their design choices with poise before parents and peers.

🎓 Outcome: Demo Day Pitch
