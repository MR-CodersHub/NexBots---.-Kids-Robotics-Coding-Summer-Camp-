Project of the Month

The Obstacle-Avoiding Rover

Developed during Week 3 of camp by 11-year-old camper Tobi. The robot uses pulse-echo timing in microseconds to measure proximity to obstacles, calculate escape pathways, and turn on a dime.

// Distance pulse calculation
digitalWrite(trigPin, HIGH);
delayMicroseconds(10);
duration = pulseIn(echoPin, HIGH);
distance = (duration * 0.034) / 2;
if (distance < 20) { reverseAndTurn(); }
Grade: A+ (Honors) • Verified by Mentor David Kalu
Educational Journey

How Campers Go from Idea to Demo Day

We don't hand children pre-assembled toys. They build their creations through a disciplined 5-stage maker pipeline.

1. Brainstorm

Sketching concept diagrams on graph paper & defining user rules.

2. Schematic

Mapping circuit pins, power voltages, resistors & ground lines.

3. Code Sprint

Writing the algorithmic loops, if/else triggers & variables.

4. Arena Test

Field trials on the camp arena course & real-time sensor tuning.

5. Pitch Demo

Presenting live to parents and guest engineers at Demo Day.
