Why CodeCampus Summer Camp?

Nurturing Young Innovators, Not Just Screen Users

At CodeCampus International, we believe every child has the innate curiosity to be an inventor. Rather than letting children spend their summer in passive screen consumption, our summer camp immerses them in the thrilling world of physical creation.

Through our experiential, project-based curriculum, campers don’t just memorize code. They design video games they can share with friends, wire obstacle-avoiding rovers from scratch, and build smart sensors that solve real problems.

✨ Learn by Doing

80% of every session is practical, hands-on construction and debugging.

🛡️ Safe & Supportive

Certified STEM coaches, child protection protocols, and secure environment.

A Day In Camp

The Daily Camp Experience

Every 3-hour morning is structured to balance focused concentration, active experimentation, and high-energy fun.

09:00 – 09:30 AM

Ignition & Brain Teaser

Campers arrive, set up their workstations, and kick off with an unplugged algorithmic logic puzzle or robotics challenge of the day.

09:30 – 10:45 AM

Coding & Software Lab

Guided mentor demonstrations followed by individual and pair-coding time building game mechanics, debugging logic, and writing Python script.

10:45 – 11:15 AM

Snack & STEM Energizer

Healthy snacks, social bonding, physical movement, and hands-on team building games that spark creativity and collaboration.

11:15 – 12:00 PM

Robotics Maker Tinkering

Hardware assembly, wiring breadboards, testing robot sensors on the camp arena track, followed by daily show-and-tell reflections.
